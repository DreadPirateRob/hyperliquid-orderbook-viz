# TapeSurf order-book visual catalogue

**Scope.** Live free-tier inspection of [TapeSurf App](https://tapesurf.com/app) on 2026-09-20, with the market selector reduced to **Binance BTC / USDT** and **Single** mode. This is a visual reference, not an implementation specification. The official [Order Book guide](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook) (updated 2026-03-16) is the authority for semantics that cannot be proven from a screenshot.

**Evidence.** The capture set spans more than two minutes and includes two 4-frame sequences with nominal 200 ms gaps: [03](./tapesurf/03-rapid-0ms.png)–[06](./tapesurf/06-rapid-600ms.png) and [12](./tapesurf/12-binance-rapid-0ms.png)–[15](./tapesurf/15-binance-rapid-600ms.png). The latter is the selected Binance/Singe view; [11](./tapesurf/11-binance-single-mode.png) is the clean reference capture. Settings and selector evidence are [07](./tapesurf/07-order-book-settings.png), [08](./tapesurf/08-market-selector.png), [17](./tapesurf/17-settings-render-performance.png), and [18](./tapesurf/18-settings-item-height-fps.png).

## Layout

- **Rendering split:** the live app exposes two `<canvas>` surfaces (the historical heatmap and the order-book pane), both with a `WebGL2RenderingContext`; the book's price/size/profile rows are therefore not DOM rows. Its controls, tabs, selectors, and settings panel are DOM/SVG. This was inspected in the live [app](https://tapesurf.com/app) and is visible as the continuous rendered book in [11](./tapesurf/11-binance-single-mode.png). Do not infer accessible table semantics from this layout.
- **Pane geometry:** in the 1440 × 1000 capture, the right-hand book pane is about 504 CSS px wide after the board split; a compact header and control strip sit above a dense, fixed-height vertical ladder ([11](./tapesurf/11-binance-single-mode.png)). This is board-dependent geometry, not a recommended component width.
- **Per-level reading order:** on each side of the divider, the left column is the grouped price; immediately right is a narrow coloured heat cell; then exact size; then a horizontal order block. A thin, angular cumulative-depth outline and optional translucent fill occupy the far-right profile area. This matches the guide's explicit grid anatomy—grouped price, heatmap bar, exact volume, and ruler-scaled volume bar ([Order Book guide: grid](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#order-book-grid))—and is clear in [11](./tapesurf/11-binance-single-mode.png).
- **Spread/current-price row:** TapeSurf separates asks and bids with a strong horizontal cyan line and cyan current-price label. The official wording is **last traded price**, with a depth-delta value below it—not a generic “best line” ([Current Price & Depth Delta](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#current-price--depth-delta)); see [12](./tapesurf/12-binance-rapid-0ms.png). This distinction matters when borrowing the layout for a feed with a separate BBO.
- **Cumulative labels:** the stair-step profile ends at labelled cumulative values on the profile side; the documented interactive label appears on the right when hovering a level ([Hover States](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#hover-states)). The official [depth-ruler anatomy](https://tapesurf.com/guides/orderbook/depth-rulers.png) places the cumulative ask/bid values adjacent to the corresponding stair-step endpoints. The live non-hover capture shows the same endpoint-side treatment ([12](./tapesurf/12-binance-rapid-0ms.png)).
- **Round-number emphasis:** with **Dimmed Text**, TapeSurf highlights every `grouping × 10` price and mutes the rest; e.g. grouping 10 emphasises every 100 price units ([Dimmed Text](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#dimmed-text)). The visual effect is visible in the muted price ladder behind the bright major references in [18](./tapesurf/18-settings-item-height-fps.png). This is a regular orientation rhythm, not a signal that a level changed.

## Feel

- **Palette, measured from the live app's computed CSS variables:** background `#121215`, primary text `#fffcf0`, asks `#ff1919` with secondary `#ffd311`, and bids `#00ffda` with secondary `#30ff76` ([app](https://tapesurf.com/app); [11](./tapesurf/11-binance-single-mode.png)). The screenshot shows the perceived rendering as deep red/amber above and cyan/green below, with warm/cool translucent profile fills. The CSS values are global theme variables, so they establish the palette but not a promise about every shader blend.
- **Intensity and width encode the same local reference:** the largest order _inside_ the two depth rulers receives maximum brightness; other in-range orders scale proportionally. The largest in-range order also sets maximum order-block width ([Depth Rulers](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#depth-rulers)). This produces the vivid amber/cyan walls and subdued small cells in [12](./tapesurf/12-binance-rapid-0ms.png), rather than treating raw size as an absolute colour.
- **Outlier colour:** TapeSurf’s **historical Order Book Heatmap** (the left widget, not the right live ladder) documents that orders above the sensitivity control’s maximum threshold receive a special accent colour ([Heatmap visual structure](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook-heatmap#price-chart-with-order-flow); [Heatmap Sensitivity](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook-heatmap#heatmap-sensitivity)). That yellow accent is visible in the left heatmap in [01](./tapesurf/01-app-initial.png). The Order Book guide says its brighter cells mark the largest in-range positions, but does **not** document a separate numeric “outlier flip” threshold for individual ladder cells. Treat the two mechanisms as distinct.
- **Depth rulers:** there are two horizontal rulers: one through the asks and one through the bids. Each shows cumulative volume at its endpoint; they set the range used for brightness, bar width, and depth delta ([Depth Rulers](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#depth-rulers); [official annotated image](https://tapesurf.com/guides/orderbook/depth-rulers.png)). In the live book they read as fine horizontal rules against an otherwise dark canvas ([12](./tapesurf/12-binance-rapid-0ms.png)).
- **Beyond a ruler:** orders outside the boundaries remain visible but do not participate in either brightness or width normalisation ([Depth Rulers](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#depth-rulers)). The guide does not describe a separate opacity/dimming transform beyond the rulers, and the capture cannot distinguish such a transform from low relative size. Do not claim a beyond-ruler dimming rule from the screenshot alone.

## Motion

- **Live cadence evidence:** the Binance sequence [12](./tapesurf/12-binance-rapid-0ms.png) → [13](./tapesurf/13-binance-rapid-200ms.png) shows the centre separator's displayed price change from 81,120 to 81,125 while remaining in the same screen band. Values, bar lengths, and the depth staircase change in the sequence through [15](./tapesurf/15-binance-rapid-600ms.png). This is consistent with the selected **Levels** alignment, documented as centring on the tightest-spread/median BBO levels for stable comparison ([Align Orderflow By](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#align-orderflow-by)).
- **Profile shape:** the cumulative profile is an orthogonal stair-step outline with optional gradient fill, not a smoothed curve ([Cumulative / Depth Line](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#cumulative-depth-line)); it redraws to a different step shape between the 200 ms captures ([12](./tapesurf/12-binance-rapid-0ms.png), [13](./tapesurf/13-binance-rapid-200ms.png)).
- **Ruler motion:** dragging either ruler moves both symmetrically to keep equal depth and rescales intensity, bar widths, and delta in real time ([Adjusting Depth Rulers](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#adjusting-depth-rulers)). There is no numeric ruler-distance setting in the observed panel ([18](./tapesurf/18-settings-item-height-fps.png)); distance is a direct-manipulation gesture.
- **Level lifecycle / interpolation:** the 200 ms captures prove that values and geometry change, but they do not isolate a single level appearing, growing, shrinking, or vanishing. Neither the guide nor the public UI gives an easing curve or duration. Do not turn the visually sharp snapshots into a claimed transition model.
- **Trade response:** no per-trade flash/pulse was observed in the order-book pane during the sampled live period, and the order-book guide does not document one. The separate historical heatmap/footprint product visualises executed trades, which is not evidence of a ladder trade flash ([Order Book Heatmap overview](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook-heatmap)).
- **Measured duration:** screenshots were spaced at nominal 200 ms, but no intermediate tween state was identifiable. An easing duration or curve is therefore **not measurable from this evidence**. The exposed FPS cap can change visual refresh only; TapeSurf says it still captures all market data ([Max FPS](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#max-fps)).

## Settings

- The live panel exposes **Mode** (Single, Aggregated, Compact), **Grouping**, **Align Orderflow By**, **Heatmap Mode**, Turbo, volume histogram, Cumulative, Gradient, Individual, Dimmed Text, Item Height, and Max FPS ([07](./tapesurf/07-order-book-settings.png), [18](./tapesurf/18-settings-item-height-fps.png)). Selecting only Binance BTC/USDT is recorded in [08](./tapesurf/08-market-selector.png); the confirmed Single-mode book is [11](./tapesurf/11-binance-single-mode.png).
- **Grouping:** the UI supplies 0.1–2500 options ([07](./tapesurf/07-order-book-settings.png)). Semantically, it rounds down and combines nearby levels; lower values give more detail and higher values broader rows ([Grouping](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#grouping)). It also supports a direct row-drag quick adjustment ([Quick Grouping Adjustment](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#quick-grouping-adjustment)); an attempted canvas drag changed the live control from 10 to 50 in [16](./tapesurf/16-rulers-narrowed.png), confirming that this gesture has a large, immediate visual effect.
- **Alignment:** None keeps books independent; Center uses a static best-bid/ask midpoint; Last Price follows the most recent trade; Levels uses the tightest-spread/median levels and is the documented default ([Align Orderflow By](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#align-orderflow-by)). The observed setting is Levels ([17](./tapesurf/17-settings-render-performance.png)).
- **Heatmap mode:** Cross Exchange uses one scale across venues; Individual uses one scale per book ([Heatmap Mode](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#heatmap-mode)). The live panel is set to Cross Exchange even after selecting one market ([18](./tapesurf/18-settings-item-height-fps.png)); that setting is only materially different in multi-book views.
- **FPS:** the dropdown has 30, 60, and Unlimited ([07](./tapesurf/07-order-book-settings.png)); it is shown as Unlimited in [18](./tapesurf/18-settings-item-height-fps.png). The guide says this caps visual refresh, not market-data capture ([Max FPS](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#max-fps)).
- **Ruler distance:** no numeric input was exposed. The documented control is dragging the ask or bid ruler, which adjusts both symmetrically ([Adjusting Depth Rulers](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#adjusting-depth-rulers)).

## Do-not-copy

- Do not copy the literal red/amber/cyan/green palette or TapeSurf branding. Borrow the **semantic separation**—side hue, intensity for local relative size, and a quieter background—then map it to the project’s own dark-theme palette and accessible text contrast ([11](./tapesurf/11-binance-single-mode.png)).
- Do not use TapeSurf’s **last-trade** separator as our “best line.” The project has a distinct BBO and a microprice ribbon; conflating them would misrepresent their producers ([Current Price & Depth Delta](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#current-price--depth-delta), [project glossary](../../CONTEXT.md#presentation)).
- Do not reproduce the hidden-looking vertical row-drag grouping gesture without an explicit affordance or undo. It changed grouping from 10 to 50 during this inspection ([16](./tapesurf/16-rulers-narrowed.png)); a similar gesture risks colliding with ruler and inspection interactions.
- Do not copy an unsourced fade, row slide, trade flash, or profile spring. The evidence supports live redraws, not TapeSurf’s interpolation parameters ([12](./tapesurf/12-binance-rapid-0ms.png)–[15](./tapesurf/15-binance-rapid-600ms.png)).
- Do not describe a higher-saturation historical heatmap cell as an order-book ladder lifecycle event. The documented accent threshold belongs to the heatmap’s sensitivity range, while the ladder’s intensity is normalised by its rulers ([Heatmap Sensitivity](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook-heatmap#heatmap-sensitivity), [Depth Rulers](https://tapesurf.com/learn/orderbook#depth-rulers)).

## Unresolved

- The exact visual lifecycle of one identified level—appear, grow, shrink, vanish—is not determinable from the live captures because several levels changed simultaneously and no first-party transition specification was found.
- The cumulative profile’s interpolation algorithm, easing curve, and duration are not documented and no identifiable intermediate state was captured at 200 ms spacing.
- A per-trade pulse/flash in the order-book ladder was neither observed nor documented; the heatmap footprint feature is a separate surface.
- The exact per-cell shader mapping from order size to the ask/bid primary-versus-secondary CSS colours is not published. The documented semantics establish relative intensity and ruler normalisation, not the colour-transfer formula.
- TapeSurf documents that outside-ruler orders stay visible and do not affect scaling, but does not specify an independent dimming/opacity rule. The captures cannot reliably separate such a rule from normalised low size.
